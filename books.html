Books

What I’m reading, what I’ve finished, and what stayed with me.

Currently Reading

Dream Psychology: Psychoanalysis for Beginners

Sigmund Freud

An introduction to dreams, the unconscious mind, symbolism, and wish fulfillment. It treats dreams as expressions of thoughts and desires that may not be obvious while we are awake.

Finished Books

5 titles

Pride and Prejudice

Jane Austen

A sharp story about first impressions, pride, social expectations, and personal growth. Beneath the romance is a clever look at how quickly people judge one another.

The Alchemist

Ben Jonson

The 1610 satirical play—not Paulo Coelho’s novel.

A witty satire about greed, deception, false expertise, and the willingness to believe almost anything when wealth is promised.

The Old Man and the Sea

Ernest Hemingway

Santiago’s struggle with the marlin becomes a compact story about endurance, dignity, pride, defeat, and continuing to fight.

Of Mice and Men

John Steinbeck

George and Lennie hold onto a simple dream in an unforgiving world. Their friendship gives the story its warmth and makes its tragedy land harder.

Animal Farm

George Orwell

A political allegory about power, propaganda, corruption, inequality, and the way revolutions can betray the ideals that began them.
